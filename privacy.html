SharpPaid privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

SharpPaid helps tradespeople and small businesses quote, invoice and get paid. This policy explains what personal information SharpPaid collects, why, who else handles it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

SharpPaid opens soon, and this policy applies from the day it opens. Until then, the only personal information we receive is what you send us by email, such as a request for early access, which we use only to answer you.

Who we are

SharpPaid is run by SharpPaid (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/888917/07, 89 Matroosberg Street, Noordheuwel, Krugersdorp, 1739, South Africa ("SharpPaid", "we").

Information Officer: Arnold Human (director), privacy@sharppaid.co.za, 066 290 6365.

Two kinds of information, two roles

  1. About you, our user: the people who sign up and use SharpPaid (business owners, admins, foremen, and accountants a business gives access to). For this information we are the responsible party under POPIA: we decide why and how it's used. An accountant a business invites sees that business's quotes, invoices and customers on its behalf.
  2. About your customers: the names, contact details, addresses, quotes and invoices you enter about the people you work for. For this information you are the responsible party and we are your operator: we process it only to provide SharpPaid to you, as set out in our terms of use. Your customers should contact you about their information; we will help you respond. The same applies to crew you add by name and WhatsApp number, who don't sign in. If you enter personal information about your customers or crew into SharpPaid, you are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary authority or lawful basis to provide that information to us and that appropriate privacy information has been provided to those individuals where required by POPIA.

What we collect about you

  • Account: your name and email address, and that you signed in. Sign-in is handled by our sign-in provider (see below); we never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Your business: name, address, phone, email, VAT and registration numbers, bank details, your trade, and your logo, as you enter them. These appear on your quotes and invoices.
  • What you do in SharpPaid: your customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, stock and team, and records such as when an email was sent or a quote was accepted.
  • Payments for Pro or Premium: your plan, your subscription's status, and each payment Payfast tells us about (its date, amount, Payfast's fee and reference). We give Payfast your name and email, so its payment page is filled in and it can send you receipts. Card details are entered on Payfast's page; we never see or store your card number.
  • Technical: our servers record basic request information (such as IP address, browser and time) to keep SharpPaid secure and working. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies; the only cookies keep you signed in and protect forms (see Cookies).

What we collect about your customers (as your operator)

  • What you enter: name, email, phone, address and VAT number, and the quotes and invoices you send them.
  • When they open a quote or invoice from the link you send: whether they accepted or declined it, when, and any reason they give.
  • When they pay by card through Yoco: that the invoice was paid. Card details go to Yoco, not to us.

Why we use it

  • To provide SharpPaid: your account, your business's records, sending your quotes, invoices and reminders, and showing your customers their documents (POPIA section 11(1)(b): needed for our contract with you).
  • To keep SharpPaid secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems (section 11(1)(f): our legitimate interests).
  • To bill Pro and Premium subscriptions, and to keep financial records as the law requires (section 11(1)(b) and (c)).
  • To tell you about changes to SharpPaid, your account, our terms or this policy. We'll only send you marketing if you've agreed to it, and you can stop it at any time.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use your customers' information for anything but providing SharpPaid to you.

Information sent outside South Africa

Some of our service providers process or store personal information outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred internationally, SharpPaid takes reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer complies with section 72 of POPIA. This may include appropriate contractual safeguards or another lawful basis permitted by POPIA. Information may also be subject to the laws of the countries in which our service providers operate.

  • Address suggestions: what you type into an address field is sent to Google to suggest matching addresses, under Google's terms and data protection commitments. Only the text typed is sent, not your other records.

How long we keep it

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting and regulatory requirements. Certain financial and transaction records may be retained for longer periods where required by applicable legislation, including tax and company record-keeping requirements.

  • While your account is open. We keep your information for as long as you use SharpPaid. When you close your account, we delete everything the law allows us to, as set out below.
  • Quotes and invoices are financial records: tax law requires them to be kept for at least five years, so they are kept for five years after they were issued, even if you close your account, unless you take a copy and ask us to delete them where the law allows.
  • Your subscription payments are our financial records, kept for five years after each payment, even if you close your account (without your name or email: only Payfast's reference).
  • When you close your account (Close account, in SharpPaid's account menu), your name and email, and everything in businesses you own alone apart from the quotes and invoices above, are deleted straight away. Your sign-in account with our sign-in provider is deleted within 30 days. Copies in our backups are deleted as the backups are replaced.

Keeping it safe

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS), and is stored encrypted in South Africa, with Google Cloud in Johannesburg, including our backups and the app's logs. Each business's records are kept apart in the database, so one business can never see another's. Access is limited to what each person needs: foremen and crew don't see prices or invoices.

At SharpPaid, only Arnold Human can reach the systems that hold your information, with two-step sign-in, and every access to the database is logged. We look at a business's records only when you ask us for help, or where the law requires it. We keep backups.

If there's a data breach

In the event of a security compromise involving personal information processed by SharpPaid, SharpPaid will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain and address the incident. Where required by POPIA, affected data subjects and the Information Regulator will be notified as soon as reasonably possible.

If a compromise affects your customers' information, we'll tell you without delay, so that you can notify them and the Information Regulator as their responsible party.

If you think your information or your SharpPaid account has been compromised, tell us straight away at privacy@sharppaid.co.za.

Your rights

You may ask us:

  • what personal information we hold about you, and for a copy (also under the Promotion of Access to Information Act);
  • to correct or delete information that's wrong, out of date or no longer needed;
  • to stop using it for a purpose you object to, such as marketing.

SharpPaid may send service-related communications necessary for the operation of the platform. Promotional or direct marketing communications will be sent in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Recipients may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Write to privacy@sharppaid.co.za. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies

SharpPaid uses only the cookies it needs: one that keeps you signed in, and one that protects your forms against misuse. Our sign-in provider, Zitadel, uses its own cookies on its sign-in page, to sign you in. None are used for tracking or advertising. Our website, sharppaid.co.za, uses no cookies.

Children

SharpPaid is for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

We'll tell you about any important change to this policy at least 30 days before it takes effect, by email to the address on your account and in SharpPaid.
