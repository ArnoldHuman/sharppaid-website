From quote to money in the bank

Six steps, from the driveway to your bank account. SharpPaid handles the paperwork in between.

  1. 01

    Quote from your phone

    Price the job on site and send the quote on WhatsApp or by email before you've left the driveway.

  2. 02

    Accepted with one tap

    Your customer opens the quote on their phone and accepts it. Ask for a deposit up front, so you're not paying for materials out of your own pocket.

  3. 03

    Invoice the moment you're done

    Turn the quote into an invoice in one tap. VAT-registered? 15% VAT and proper tax invoices are handled for you.

  4. 04

    Easy to pay

    Every invoice can have a "Pay now" button for card payments, plus your bank details with the invoice number as the reference.

  5. 05

    Reminders do the chasing

    When an invoice is late, SharpPaid sends a friendly reminder for you. No awkward phone calls.

  6. 06

    Know who owes you

    One screen shows what's outstanding, what's overdue and what's been paid this month.
