Everything a small trade business runs on

Jobs and scheduling

A simple two-week schedule. Add the customer, address and time; tap the address for directions. Mark jobs in progress or done as you go.

Quotes and tax invoices

Quote from the job, turn an accepted quote into an invoice, and share the PDF on WhatsApp or by email. VAT-registered? 15% VAT and proper tax invoices are handled for you.

Stock in every van

Know what's in the storeroom and each bakkie. Record what was used on which job, move stock between vans, and see a warning when stock runs low.

Your crew

Invite a helper with a link on WhatsApp. Assign jobs, and let your crew see their day and update their jobs. They don't see your prices or invoices.

Customers

Names, phone numbers, addresses and VAT numbers in one place, ready for every job, quote and invoice. SharpPaid warns you when an emailed invoice bounces.

Made for your phone

Works in your phone's browser on site and on a computer in the office. Nothing to install.

Working alone?

SharpPaid stays out of your way: no crew screens, no setup. Add a customer, book the job, send the invoice.

Running a team?

Give each person the right access. Owners and admins handle money and planning; crew see their jobs and record the work.

Your business's information stays yours

  • Each business's data is kept strictly separate.
  • Secure sign-in, with two-step verification available.
  • Issued invoices can't be changed afterwards, so your records stay trustworthy.
