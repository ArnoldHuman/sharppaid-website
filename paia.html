SharpPaid PAIA manual

Prepared under section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2 of 2000, as amended.

Compiled: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. About this manual

The Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA) gives everyone the right to ask for records a company holds, where they need them to exercise or protect a right. This manual explains what records SharpPaid (Pty) Ltd keeps, how to ask for them, and how SharpPaid processes personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

This manual helps you to:

  • see which of our records are available without a formal request;
  • understand how to ask for a record, from a description of the subjects we keep records on;
  • see which records we keep because other laws require them;
  • contact our Information Officer;
  • find the Information Regulator's guide on how to use PAIA;
  • see why we process personal information, about whom, who receives it, whether it goes outside South Africa, and how we keep it safe.

In this manual, "Information Officer" means our Information Officer under PAIA and POPIA, and "the Regulator" means the Information Regulator.

3. The Information Regulator's guide

The Regulator has updated and made available, under section 10(1) of PAIA, a guide on how to use PAIA and POPIA (the "Guide"), for anyone who wants to exercise a right under them. It's available in each of the official languages and in braille. It describes:

  • the objects of PAIA and POPIA;
  • the contact details of every public body's Information Officer, and of every Deputy Information Officer;
  • how to ask for a record of a public body, and of a private body such as SharpPaid;
  • the help available from public bodies' Information Officers and from the Regulator;
  • every remedy in law, including internal appeals, complaints to the Regulator and applications to court;
  • the duty to compile a manual like this one, and how to get one;
  • the voluntary disclosure of records, the fees for requests, and the PAIA Regulations.

You can get the Guide:

  • from the Regulator's website, inforegulator.org.za/paia;
  • by asking our Information Officer;
  • by inspecting or copying it at our registered office, in English and Afrikaans, during normal business hours (please arrange a time with us first), or at the Regulator's office.

The Regulator: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191; 010 023 5200; enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

  • Income Tax Act, 58 of 1962, and Tax Administration Act, 28 of 2011: tax returns, and the records behind them.

6. The records we hold

  • The company: registration documents and memorandum of incorporation, the securities register, directors' resolutions, beneficial ownership filings, and correspondence with CIPC.
  • Finance and tax: accounting records, bank statements, tax returns and correspondence with SARS, and the B-BBEE certificate.
  • Agreements: with our service providers (hosting, sign-in, email, payments) and their data processing terms.
  • People who use SharpPaid: accounts (name, email, the days they signed in), the businesses they belong to and their roles, their plans, subscriptions and the payments Payfast tells us about, and our emails with them.
  • What businesses keep in SharpPaid: their customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, stock and team, and when emails were sent and quotes were accepted. We hold these as each business's operator: a request for them should go to the business itself, and we'll help it answer.
  • Running SharpPaid: the app's code, security and request logs, backups, and records of who looked at what in the database.

Holding a record listed here doesn't mean we'll give access to it: section 8 explains why access can be refused.

7. How to ask for a record

  1. Fill in Form 2 ("Request for access to record", regulation 7 of the PAIA Regulations). It's on the Regulator's website, inforegulator.org.za/paia, or we'll send it to you.
  2. Email it to privacy@sharppaid.co.za, or deliver or post it to our address above. Describe the records clearly, say how you'd like to receive them, and explain which right you need them to exercise or protect. If you ask on someone else's behalf, include proof that you may.
  3. We'll tell you if a request fee is payable before we start. There's none if you ask for personal information about yourself. Fees for searching, preparing and copying records are set by the PAIA Regulations, and we'll tell you the amount and any deposit before we do the work.
  4. We'll decide within 30 days of receiving the request, and tell you why if we refuse. We may extend this once, by up to 30 days, if the request is large or needs others to be consulted, and will tell you if we do.

If you only want to know what personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, you don't need this form: write to privacy@sharppaid.co.za, as our privacy policy explains.

8. Why a request may be refused

PAIA (Part 3, Chapter 4) requires or allows us to refuse access to protect, among others:

  • another person's privacy, including information about our users and their customers;
  • someone else's commercial information or trade secrets, or information given to us in confidence;
  • people's safety, and the security of SharpPaid's systems;
  • records privileged in legal proceedings;
  • our own commercial information, research and trade secrets.

A request that's clearly frivolous or vexatious, or would take up an unreasonable amount of work, may also be refused.

9. If you're not satisfied

There's no internal appeal at a private company. If we refuse your request, or you don't agree with a fee or an extension, you may complain to the Information Regulator within 180 days, on its Form 5 ("Complaint form", from inforegulator.org.za/paia), or apply to court. The same applies if we haven't answered within 30 days.

10. Personal information we process (POPIA)

Why we process it: to provide SharpPaid to the businesses that use it; to keep it secure and working; to bill Pro and Premium subscriptions; to keep the records the law requires; and to tell users about changes to SharpPaid, their accounts and our terms.

Whose information, and what:

  • People who use SharpPaid: name, email, the days they signed in, their businesses and roles, and their plans and subscription payments (not card numbers, which go to Payfast).
  • Their businesses: name, address, phone, email, VAT and registration numbers, bank details, trade and logo.
  • Their customers and crew (as the businesses' operator): names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, VAT numbers, and their quotes and invoices, and whether they opened, accepted or paid them.
  • Anyone who writes to us: their name, email and what they wrote.
  • Our own suppliers: their contact and banking details.

Sent outside South Africa: as the table shows, sign-in, email, address suggestions and our own email are handled outside South Africa. We do this under section 72 of POPIA, with the providers' data protection terms. SharpPaid's database, backups and logs stay in South Africa.

How we keep it safe: everything travels encrypted and is stored encrypted, in South Africa. Each business's records are kept apart in the database, so one business can't see another's. Only the director can reach the systems that hold personal information, with two-step sign-in, and every access to the database is logged. Backups are kept.
